01Investigating an organic traffic decline+

Question: Which pages, queries, devices, or markets account for the change?

Planned method: Validate tracking, compare appropriate periods in GA4 and Search Console, segment losses, and inspect affected pages for indexation and content issues.

Evidence to add: Anonymized data, dates, hypotheses tested, findings, recommended actions, and follow-up measurements.

Status: outline only. Site, baseline, findings, and outcomes are awaiting verification.

02Prioritizing a technical SEO audit+

Question: What technical barriers prevent important pages from being discovered and indexed?

Planned method: Review robots directives, sitemaps, status codes, canonical tags, internal links, structured data, and Core Web Vitals. Prioritize by impact, confidence, and effort.

Evidence to add: Audit scope, crawl exports, reproducible issues, a prioritized backlog, and validation after any fixes.

Status: outline only. No audit findings or implementation outcomes are claimed.

03Building an HVAC Answer Map+

Question: How can HVAC content answer customer questions across the search journey?

Planned method: Research questions about installation, maintenance, repairs, and costs; group by intent; map topics to existing or proposed pages; define internal links and measurement.

Evidence to add: Verified keyword sources, a topic map, content briefs, publication dates, and measured search performance.

Status: proposed project. Website, market, deliverables, and results are awaiting confirmation.
